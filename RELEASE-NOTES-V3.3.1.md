# Quantum YiJing® v3.3.1 — Release Notes

v3.3.1 adds two affiliate-programme rules:

- Affiliate membership is renewable every 12 months.
- A customer's first successfully paid referred purchase establishes a 12-month customer attribution.

Referral click window remains 30 days.

Affiliate code remains permanent even if membership expires. Historical and already-earned commissions remain preserved. New commissions require active affiliate membership.

Planned renewal emails:
- 30 days before expiry
- 7 days before expiry
- expiry-day notice

Planned attribution policy:
- direct visits do not overwrite valid attribution
- last valid affiliate referral wins when a later affiliate referral legitimately replaces the current attribution
