Quantum YiJing® Affiliate Programme
Terms & Conditions
量子易经联盟计划条款与条件

This detailed document supplements the summary shown on the Affiliate Programme page. / 本文件为联盟计划页面摘要条款的完整说明。

1. Eligibility and Approval / 资格与批准

Participation begins only after QY approval and issue of an active Affiliate Code. / 只有在 QY 批准并发出有效联盟代码后，联盟资格才正式生效。

2. Affiliate Code / 联盟代码

Each approved affiliate receives one permanent QY Affiliate Code for eligible QY offerings. / 每位获批准联盟会员获得一个永久 QY 联盟代码，可用于推广符合资格的 QY 项目。

3. Commission / 佣金

The default rate is currently 20%; product-specific or affiliate-specific rates may differ. Historical rates are snapshotted per sale. / 默认佣金目前为 20%，个别产品或联盟会员可有不同佣金率；每笔交易保留当时佣金率。

4. Eligible Sales / 合资格销售

Only successfully paid, affiliate-enabled sales qualify. Refunded, cancelled, failed, fraudulent or chargeback transactions may be excluded or reversed. / 仅成功付款且已启用联盟佣金的交易可获得佣金。退款、取消、失败、欺诈或拒付交易可被排除或撤销佣金。

5. Referral Window / 推荐有效期

The normal initial referral window is 30 days. / 首次推荐有效期一般为 30 天。

6. Customer Attribution / 客户归属

After the first successfully paid referred purchase, the customer may remain attributed to that affiliate for 12 months, provided the affiliate remains active. / 客户完成首次成功付款的推荐购买后，可归属于该联盟会员 12 个月，但联盟会员须保持有效资格。

7. Attribution Changes / 归属变更

Direct visits do not normally erase attribution. QY may apply the last valid affiliate referral wins rule. / 客户直接访问一般不会删除原归属；QY 可采用最后一次有效推荐优先规则。

8. Membership and Renewal / 会员资格与续期

Membership is currently valid for 12 months and renewable annually. Earned commissions remain payable after expiry, but new commissions are not earned while inactive. / 会员资格目前为 12 个月并须每年续期；到期后已赚取佣金仍可支付，但非活跃期间不产生新佣金。

9. Monthly Payout / 每月付款

Approved commissions are normally paid monthly by bank transfer. / 获批准佣金一般按月通过银行转账支付。

10. Customer Information / 客户资料

Limited customer information may be shown only for commission reconciliation and must not be used for unrelated marketing or disclosure. / 有限客户资料只可用于佣金核对，不得用于无关营销或披露。

11. Bank Information / 银行资料

Bank information is used for payout administration. / 银行资料仅用于佣金付款管理。

12. Prohibited Conduct / 禁止行为

No false claims, misleading advertising, impersonation, referral manipulation or fraudulent transactions. / 禁止虚假声明、误导广告、冒充 QY、操纵推荐或欺诈交易。

13. Suspension or Termination / 暂停或终止

QY may suspend or terminate participation for rule breaches, fraud, abuse, reputational risk or inaccurate information. / QY 可因违反规则、欺诈、滥用、声誉风险或不准确资料暂停或终止联盟资格。

14. Programme Changes / 计划变更

QY may update programme rules, rates, eligible offerings and procedures. / QY 可更新计划规则、佣金率、合资格项目及程序。

15. Acceptance / 接受条款

Submitting an application and accepting the terms constitutes agreement to these Terms & Conditions. / 提交申请并勾选同意即表示接受本《条款与条件》。
