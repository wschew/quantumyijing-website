# Quantum YiJing v2.9 — Sales & Commerce CRM

## Added
- New Sales & Commerce module in the private Academy Operating System.
- Product catalogue management for courses, memberships, consultations, ebooks, digital, physical and event products.
- Multi-channel product configuration: Website/SenangPay, Google Play Books/Google, manual and marketplace channels.
- Manual order creation with campaign and affiliate attribution.
- Sales summary: active products, orders, paid/external orders, pending orders and recorded revenue.
- Channel, payment-provider and payment-status analytics.
- Existing v2.7 products/orders/payments schema is reused; no destructive migration.

## Intentionally not activated yet
- Live SenangPay checkout/callback.
- Automated affiliate commissions.
- Stock/fulfilment/shipping.
- Customer self-service portal.

These are safer follow-on releases after the v2.9 order ledger is verified in Preview.
