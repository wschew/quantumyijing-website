# Quantum YiJing v2.5.0 — CRM Intelligence & Follow-up

v2.5 builds on the v2.4 Academy Operating System with a more actionable lead-management workflow.

## New
- Lead priority: Hot, Warm, Normal, Low.
- Preferred contact method: Any, Email, WhatsApp, Phone.
- Next-action field and searchable tags.
- Priority filtering in the CRM.
- Follow-up intelligence dashboard: overdue, due today and next seven days.
- Quick follow-up buttons for tomorrow, +3 days, +7 days and mark contacted.
- Automatic follow-up timeline entries.
- CSV export now includes priority, preferred contact, next action and tags.

## Existing systems retained
- Cloudflare D1 enquiry database.
- Secure ADMIN_TOKEN authentication.
- Resend customer and Academy emails.
- Student conversion and Student ID generation.
- Activity timeline and lifecycle management.
