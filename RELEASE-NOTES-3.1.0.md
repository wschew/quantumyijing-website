# Release Notes — v3.1

## Unified Payment & Accounting Foundation
- Payment-provider-agnostic accounting model
- Separate gross sales, provider/platform fees, net amount and bank received
- Bank transfer verification workflow
- External-platform settlement workflow
- Google Play Books compatible accounting records
- Customer receipt issuer tracking
- New Payment & Accounting table in Admin
- New Record Payment dialog
- Receipts database foundation for future automatic PDF receipts
- Existing CRM, Students, Marketing, Products, Orders and dynamic product funnel retained

Live SenangPay callbacks are intentionally deferred to the next payment-integration stage.
