Operations Boundary

  • This page is a global subscription register and routing console.
  • It does not directly change subscription lifecycle state.
  • Lifecycle changes remain in Subscription Lifecycle Administration.
  • Manual renewals remain in Subscription Renewal Administration.
  • Orders, payments and accounting remain the financial source of truth.
  • No automatic renewal capability is introduced by A6G.
