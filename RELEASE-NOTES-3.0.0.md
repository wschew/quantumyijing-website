# Quantum YiJing® v3.0 — Automated Product & Sales Funnel

v3.0 turns the v2.9 product catalogue into a public, database-driven sales funnel.

- Dynamic D1-driven product routes at `/product/<slug>`.
- YJ12 is the first real live pilot.
- Automatic early-bird pricing based on Malaysia date.
- EN / 中文 public product page.
- Product dates, times, Zoom delivery, instructor, descriptions and banner image.
- Automatic slug generation in Academy Operating System product editor.
- Active products receive a public **View** link from Sales & Commerce.
- Registration form captures UTM/campaign/affiliate attribution.
- Website registrations create a CRM enquiry plus a Pending order using the effective price.
- Existing Google Play Books / external-marketplace payment paths remain external.
- SenangPay live payment is intentionally not enabled until v3.1.
